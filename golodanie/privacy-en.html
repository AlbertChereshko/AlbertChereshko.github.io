Privacy Policy of the Golodanie app (Intermittent Fasting)

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what data the Golodanie app (the "app") collects and stores, why it is needed and how you can manage it. If this English version and the Russian version differ, the Russian version applies.

Developer: Albert Chereshko

Contact: achereshko@ukr.net

In short

  • Everything you enter is stored on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google, a copy of this data is stored in Google Firebase. Only your account can access it.
  • We show no ads, use no third-party analytics, and do not sell or share your data for marketing.
  • The app does not replace a doctor. Information about fasting phases and BMI is general.

What data the app processes

Data you enter yourself:

  • fasts: start and end time, the chosen plan (for example, 16:8);
  • water: time and amount;
  • coffee: time, drink and approximate caffeine;
  • height and daily weight, the calculated body mass index (BMI);
  • settings: fasting plan, appearance, language, caffeine limit.

Weight, height and fasting records are health information. We use them only to show you statistics in the app.

If you sign in with Google (optional):

  • your Google account ID, email address and name, which Google provides at sign-in. The address and name are used only to show which account you are signed in with.

The app does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Where the data is stored

On your phone. Data is stored in the app's storage on your device. Without signing in, it is not sent anywhere.

In the cloud (only after signing in with Google). So that your data is available on another device and survives a phone change, the app keeps a copy in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), provided by Google LLC. Data is sent over an encrypted connection and stored in a space only your account can access. How Google processes data in Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Notifications and widgets

Notifications about the end of a fast and the end of the "I'm hungry" countdown are created on the phone and do not pass through our servers. Home screen widgets show information from the app. If you don't want this information visible on the lock screen or home screen, turn off the notifications or remove the widgets.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • On the phone, data is kept until you delete it. You can delete a single fast in the history. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings erases everything.
  • In the cloud, data is kept while you have an account in the app. In the app you can sign out and delete your account together with all cloud data. You can also ask us to delete your account and data without the app: how to do it.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except:

  • Google LLC as the sign-in and cloud storage provider (Firebase), only if you signed in with Google;
  • when disclosure is required by law.

Children

The app is intended for adults. Intermittent fasting is not recommended for children and teenagers without medical supervision. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

Medical disclaimer

The app does not diagnose or give medical advice. Talk to a doctor before you start fasting, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, an eating disorder or a chronic illness, or take medication.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page and announce significant changes in the app.

Contact
